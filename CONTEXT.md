# Verbatim

An engine that interviews a person before it writes a LinkedIn post in their
voice, and refuses to write anything they did not say. The engine is shared and
in English; everything about one person stays with that person.

## Language

**Material**:
Everything the engine is allowed to draw a fact from about one person: who they
are, what they can prove, their voice, their pillars, their idea bank. One
file, held by the person, attached to a conversation.
_Avoid_: profile (that is one section of it), instance, account, dossier

**Ledger**:
The record of posts that exist, one entry per post, carrying its pillar, its
format, its state and its measurement. Lives inside the material.
_Avoid_: index, store, history, log

**Corpus**:
The text of posts that are already published, kept as evidence of how the
person actually writes. Where a post was drafted stops mattering once it is
out, so this covers posts written here and posts written before the engine
existed.
_Avoid_: posts, archive, samples

**Sheet**:
The validation sheet: angle, concrete elements, strong moment, conviction,
first line. Approved by the person before a single line is drafted, and the
gate the whole promise rests on.
_Avoid_: brief, outline, summary, plan

**Anchor**:
The link between one claim in a draft and the exact sentence that backs it,
naming which source holds that sentence. A claim with no anchor is a claim the
engine invented.
_Avoid_: citation, reference, source, proof

**Pack**:
The wording, style lists and market rules for one language. Never a translation
of another pack: the categories are shared, the lists that fill them are not.
_Avoid_: locale, translation, i18n

**Pillar**:
One of the three recurring subjects a person writes about, with the ratio
between them. What a post is balanced against.
_Avoid_: theme, topic, category

**Floor**:
The weakest host the engine is written against: a conversation that can receive
an attachment and return text, and cannot write a file or run code. Anything
the floor cannot hold is not part of the contract.
_Avoid_: fallback, degraded mode, minimum viable

**Tier**:
A host that can do more than the floor, such as an agent with a filesystem.
A tier adds convenience and never adds a promise.
_Avoid_: mode, level, upgrade path
